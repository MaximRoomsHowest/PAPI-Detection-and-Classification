# Model Card — PAPI Vision Detector

A concise, honest model card (audit IMP-DOC-8). The full lineage, promotion
procedure and run-by-run metrics live in [`models/MODELS.md`](../models/MODELS.md);
this card adds the intended-use, limitations and ethics framing a registry omits.

## Model details

- **Name / run**: `yolo26s-fulldata-1280` (serving slot `models/serving/best.pt`; promoted
  2026-05-31, superseding the yolo26n sequence model).
- **Architecture**: Ultralytics YOLO26s (~9.1 M params), object detection.
- **Classes**: 2 — `PAPI-Red` (0), `PAPI-White` (1). The third per-lamp
  state, *transition*, is derived geometrically from the viewing angle, not learned.
- **Inputs**: a single image / video frame (plus optional drone GPS + altitude supplied
  by the surrounding software — the angle is **not** inferred from pixels).
- **Outputs**: per-lamp bounding boxes + state, a five-state global glidepath verdict,
  and a confidence score.
- **Provenance**: SHA-256 + training-run metrics are served live at `GET /api/model`
  from `models/serving/model_card.json` (generated by
  `workflows/scripts/populate_model_metrics.py`).

## Intended use

- **Primary**: a demonstrator that proves automated PAPI detection + per-lamp
  classification is viable from drone imagery, for Intersoft Electronics and the
  Howest jury.
- **Out of scope**: this is **not** a certified safety-of-flight system. It must not be
  used as an authoritative source for approach-path guidance. Outputs are advisory and
  intended for review by qualified aviation engineers.

## Limitations & caveats

- **Daytime is the hard case** — sun glare and washed-out red/white separation; report
  day vs night metrics separately rather than letting strong night performance hide it.
- **Geometry inputs are provisional** — the rwy-06 installation height, the height datum
  for 461.37 m, the lamp-numbering convention and the commissioned set-angles are
  unconfirmed by the client; transition-boundary labels shift once they are bound.
- **ZoomCamera** frames are degraded until `calibrated_focal_px` is supplied.
- **Throughput** — ~0.4 fps on a laptop CPU; far from the "real-time on a
  resource-constrained device" requirement. That figure was measured on the previous
  yolo26n; the serving model is now the ~3.5× larger yolo26s, so CPU throughput will be
  lower and needs re-measuring. GPU / ONNX-runtime / smaller-input paths are the levers,
  and real edge-hardware numbers are still pending (`docs/edge-benchmark.md`).
- **INT8 ONNX export** fails on CPU ONNX Runtime (`ConvInteger(10)` unimplemented).

## Ethical considerations

- Aviation-safety context: a wrong "correct glidepath" verdict is more dangerous than an
  "unknown". The system favours surfacing uncertainty (an `unknown` state when fewer than
  four lamps are confidently detected) and shows the evidence behind every result.
- No personal data is processed; imagery is of runway infrastructure.
